Cross-Cutting Methods

This page ranks which named techniques recur across the most AASKAII sections. A fixed vocabulary of 16 techniques was searched independently of section boundaries, so a method's rank reflects how load-bearing it is corpus-wide, not how often any one section happens to mention it.

Overview & method Methods ranking Notes & caveats

Scope & method

This analysis uses the same corpus as the observing-mode contention map: the AASKAII book, 7 thematic sections, and roughly 189 papers. A separate pass ran 16 fixed technique keywords against the whole corpus, independent of section boundaries, to rank which methods are load-bearing across the most sections rather than specific to just one.

Sourcing follows the same rule throughout. We report a figure or claim only where a paper states it explicitly, and we source it back to that paper.

Cross-cutting methods ranking

16 fixed keywords · corpus-wide search

We searched a fixed vocabulary of 16 techniques independently of section boundaries. We then ranked each technique by how many of the 7 AASKAII sections use it as a load-bearing method rather than a passing mention. These counts are a lower bound because a 20-result search window will not surface every paper. The ranking of the top methods is nonetheless robust.

The observing-mode contention map already showed that broad observing modes, such as wide continuum imaging or commensal transient monitoring, span 5 of 6 science sections. This pass asks a narrower question: which named techniques are load-bearing? Faraday rotation and polarimetry form the only technique that is genuinely both broad and deep. It is used materially in 6 of 7 sections, not just mentioned in passing. Everything else that looks broad in the contention map, including VLBI and transients, turns out at the technique level to be a family of related but distinct methods per section rather than one shared pipeline.

Methods ranking: We chose 16 fixed keywords before searching: 8 came from the user's own examples, and 8 more span imaging, VLBI, RFI, and community-science techniques. We ran each keyword as an independent corpus-wide query with a 20-result window. The absolute paper counts are a lower bound. The full-read section passes behind both this document and the earlier contention map independently corroborate the ranking of Faraday rotation and polarimetry as the most cross-cutting technique.
