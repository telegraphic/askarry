Notes & caveats

Scope & method

This analysis uses the same corpus as the observing-mode contention map: the AASKAII book, 7 thematic sections, and roughly 189 papers. We reopened each of the 7 section research passes and asked what hard numbers the authors quote as necessary for their science.

Sourcing follows the same rule throughout. We report a figure or claim only where a paper states it explicitly, and we source it back to that paper. A gap in a table cell means the paper did not state a number for that axis. It does not mean the requirement does not exist.

Instrument requirements: Every cell is sourced from the paper's own stated numbers; none are derived or estimated. "Not stated" means the source paper reports sensitivity or resolution but does not report that particular axis. Field of view and survey speed are the most commonly omitted axes, because many science cases are pointed or triggered observations rather than survey-mode. We exclude historical 2015-book baseline figures, except where a 2026 paper cites them directly for comparison.
