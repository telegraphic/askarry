What this is

Policies on the use of AI in proposal generation within the astronomy community are still evolving. This page demonstrates how a large language model (LLM) can draft a proposal-shaped document from the AASKA II corpus, along with access to the sensitivity calculator API (made available via an MCP endpoint). The draft is not a real proposal. It is an illustrative example of what an LLM can produce when prompted with a specific task and given access to a relevant corpus of documents.

The science case, in one paragraph

Neutral hydrogen (HI) extends farther from a galaxy than any other gas component. HI also disturbs more easily than other gas components. These two properties make HI the earliest tracer of tidal stripping and ram-pressure stripping as a galaxy falls into a cluster. The proposal requests resolved 21-cm HI imaging of 12 galaxies. These galaxies sit in the outskirts and infall region of a nearby, low-mass cluster at roughly 20 Mpc. The imaging will map tidal tails, warps, and jellyfish gas tails. The proposal targets resolution and depth beyond what MeerKAT, ASKAP, and WSRT have reached.

Observation setup

The proposal requests SKA-Mid Array Assembly * (AA*) in Band 2. The observation restricts the core sub-array to baselines of 2 km or less. This restriction trades some of AA*'s full collecting area for a beam that matches the few-kpc scale of tidal features.

Data reduction & analysis plan

Standard SDP pipelines will calibrate and image the data. This step includes bandpass, flux, and phase calibration. This step also includes RFI flagging and joint deconvolution for each target.

The pipeline will produce HI moment maps at native and smoothed resolution. These maps show intensity, velocity field, and velocity dispersion.

The team will classify each target's morphology and kinematics as disc, warp, truncation, or tail. The team will compare this classification against clustercentric distance and galaxy mass.

The team will extract rotation curves for targets with regular discs. The team will compare these curves against a mass-matched field sample.

The SKA Regional Centre Network will archive the visibilities, images, and derived products.

Caveats

This page demonstrates Askarry drafting a proposal-shaped document from the corpus. It is not a vetted time-allocation request. The PI and co-I fields are placeholders. The "Fornax cluster" sample is illustrative. It is not a specific, pre-selected target list. This page did not independently re-run the sensitivity figures through the live SKAO Sensitivity Calculator. Treat every number on this page as a first-pass estimate. Check each number before you use it. The known issues logged for the rest of the atlas apply here too.
