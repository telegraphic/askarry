AASKAII Atlas

This site analyzes the science chapters of Advancing Astrophysics with the SKA II. An AI system generated the analyses. That system is a RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) pipeline. The pipeline was built from the AASKA and AASKA II documents. The pipeline also used publicly available SKA technical documentation.
This site is a prototype. AI generated most of the text on this site. The Atlas overview page explains the method in full.
