AASKAII Atlas

This site presents exploratory analyses of the science chapters of Advancing Astrophysics with the SKA II. The analyses were done using AI tools by querying a RAG (retrieval augemented generation) system built from parsing the AASKA and AASKA II documents, along with publicly-available technical information on the SKA capabilities.
