SKAO · AASKAII — Cross-Corpus Analysis, Part III

Synergy Network

The AASKAII papers state which external facilities SKAO needs for each science case. This diagram reconstructs those statements as a network linking science themes to the observatories, missions, and arrays each theme requires.

Hub facilities

Rubin/LSST and JWST carry the whole survey side

Rubin/LSST and JWST each connect to five themes: cosmology, galaxy/AGN evolution, reionization, transients, and the dark/dim universe. The papers treat both facilities as necessary for every one of these science cases.

Densest theme

Galaxy & AGN evolution pulls in almost everyone

Rubin, JWST, ALMA, and X-ray facilities all connect to this theme. The papers require panchromatic SED decomposition, combining radio, optical/NIR, submillimeter, and X-ray data, to separate star formation from AGN activity.

Isolated community

Gravitational-wave science is nearly its own island

LISA, LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA, Einstein Telescope, and CMB missions link almost exclusively to the Gravitational Waves and Pulsars themes. This community is largely separate from the galaxy-survey and cosmology side. Gaia astrometry is the only facility that crosses over between the two.

Self-contained pair

ALMA and star formation are a tight duo

The papers discuss ALMA–SKA synergy almost entirely in spectral-line and young-stellar-object work. Few papers connect ALMA to cosmology. This tie is narrow but strong; ALMA does not function as a hub facility.

Caveat

The long tail is thin because the search was, not because the science is

FAST, CHORD, DSA2000, eROSITA, and Athena each surfaced only one or two theme connections in this pass. The original search was driven by facility name rather than an exhaustive scan, so this count likely understates their real connections. The search found no confirmed edges for ngVLA or CTA/Fermi. This page marks both as open gaps.

Provenance: This diagram reconstructs a synergy-network analysis from a separate conversation. It is based on that conversation's written summary of findings, not on the original edge-weight dataset, which was not available here. Node placement and exact edge counts are an interpretive reconstruction. The summary directly states the clusters, the two hub facilities, the GW-community isolation, and the Gaia bridge. This page represents the finding accurately but is not a pixel-exact reproduction of the original graph.

Known gaps (per the original analysis): The original targeted search did not include gamma-ray facilities (CTA, Fermi) for pulsar-wind-nebula synergies. It also did not include other radio facilities (ngVLA, VLBI networks). These facilities may have real connections that this analysis does not represent.
