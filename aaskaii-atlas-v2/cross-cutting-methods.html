Cross-Cutting Methods

Sixteen techniques recur across the book, from Faraday rotation to citizen science. The table ranks them first by how many of the seven sections use them and then by how many chapters do. This ranking separates the methods that bind the science case together from those that serve a single community.

How to read the counts

The counts come from an exact scan of every indexed passage, not from search results, so they are complete. A chapter counts when the technique's search terms appear in it at least twice, and the terms are listed above. The two VLBI entries are kept apart on purpose. "VLBI" counts any use of the technique, whereas "VLBI astrometry" counts only the chapters that discuss astrometry.
