Cross-Cutting Methods

Sixteen techniques recur across the book, from Faraday rotation to citizen science. The table ranks them by the number of the seven book sections in which at least one chapter relies on them, and then by the number of chapters, which separates methods that bind the science case together from those that serve a single community.

Chapters per section

Each cell gives the number of chapters in that section that name the technique at least twice; hover a cell for the section's size.

How to read the counts

The counts come from an exact scan of every indexed chunk rather than from search results, so they are complete rather than lower bounds. A chapter counts when the technique's terms occur at least twice; the terms appear under the matrix. Two VLBI entries are kept apart deliberately: "VLBI" counts any use of the technique, while "VLBI astrometry" counts only chapters that speak of astrometric work.

What changed from v1: v1 counts were lower bounds from a 20-hit search window and merged the two VLBI keywords.
