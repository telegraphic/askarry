The AASKAII Atlas

The atlas reads the whole of Advancing Astrophysics with the SKA II and reports what the book asks of the SKA telescopes. It shows which observing modes and techniques the chapters rely on, which other facilities they pair with, and how much observing time they request and where on the sky. It also tests the stated requirements against the SKAO sensitivity calculator and the observing contexts the SKAO has defined. A script that anyone can rerun computes every figure from the book, and every quotation has been checked against the chapter it comes from.
