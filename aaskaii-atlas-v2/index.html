The AASKAII Atlas

The atlas reads the whole of Advancing Astrophysics with the SKA II and reports what the book asks of the telescopes: which observing modes and techniques it relies on, which facilities it pairs with, how much time it requests and where on the sky, and which of its stated requirements survive a check against the sensitivity calculator and the defined observing contexts. Every figure is computed from the book by a script that can be rerun, and every quotation has been checked against the chapter it is attributed to.
