Observing-Mode Map

The map counts how many chapters in each of the book's seven sections name each of the fifteen observing modes that the atlas uses as its fixed taxonomy. It measures how widely a mode is invoked across the science case; it does not measure competition for telescope time, which the time-demand page now computes from stated hour requests.

Chapters per section

Each cell gives the number of chapters in that section that name the observing mode at least twice; hover a cell for the section's size.

How to read the counts

A chapter counts toward a mode when the mode's search terms occur in it at least twice, a threshold that removes passing mentions and institutional affiliations while keeping chapters that discuss the mode in earnest. The terms behind each mode are listed below the matrix, so a reader who doubts a count can see exactly what was matched and rerun it with keyword_coverage_tool.

Version 1 presented this matrix as a contention map. That label overstated what frequency can show: a mode named by many chapters may still be cheap in hours, and a mode named by a single survey chapter may dominate the schedule. Frequency therefore appears here under its proper name, and the scheduling question moves to the page built for it.

What changed from v1: v1 compressed three sections into invented sub-themes and hand-counted from 20-result search windows; these counts are exact over every indexed chunk.
