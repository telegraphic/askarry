Open Questions

A science case is defined as much by what it admits it does not know as by what it plans to measure. This page gathers every statement in which an AASKAII chapter says that something remains unclear, debated or untested. Each statement is quoted from its chapter and has been checked against it, so you can read the book's unknowns side by side.

How to read the list

The section agents searched every chapter for the language of open problems, such as "remains unclear", "open question", "poorly understood" and "will test". They quoted the sentence that states each problem. The agent also gave each quote a short theme label to help you browse. That label is an editorial summary, whereas the quotation is the evidence. The page keeps every statement instead of grouping them into clusters, and you can use the filter to group them yourself. As a result, no question is lost at the boundary between two clusters.
