Open Questions

A science case is defined as much by what it admits it does not know as by what it plans to measure. This page gathers the statements in which AASKAII chapters say that something remains unclear, debated or untested, each quoted from the chapter and checked against it, so that the book's unknowns can be read side by side.

How to read the list

The section agents searched every chapter for the language of open problems ("remains unclear", "open question", "poorly understood", "will test") and quoted the sentence that states each one. The short theme label beside each quote was assigned by the agent to aid browsing; it is an editorial summary, whereas the quotation is the evidence. Version 1 grouped such statements into ten clusters by judgement; this version keeps every statement and lets the filter do the grouping, so that no question disappears into a cluster boundary.
