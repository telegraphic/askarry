2015 → 2026 Science Case

The 2015 book, Advancing Astrophysics with the Square Kilometre Array, was organised by science working group; its successor is organised by astrophysical theme, with the working groups reduced to a section of overviews. Comparing the two therefore means asking where each 2015 working-group topic now lives, and the matrix below answers that question by counting the 2026 chapters that treat each topic substantively.

What moved

Most 2015 topics map onto one dominant 2026 section, which confirms that the reorganisation regrouped the science case rather than discarding parts of it. Cosmology and the Epoch of Reionisation now concentrate in The Cosmos, the continuum universe in From the Milky Way to Distant Galaxies, and pulsar physics and transients in The Extreme Universe. The Hydrogen Universe, by contrast, divides almost evenly between galaxy evolution and cosmology, reflecting the separation between resolved HI studies of galaxies and HI intensity mapping of large-scale structure.

Magnetism is the clearest case of a topic that has spread rather than moved: chapters in every 2026 section treat magnetic fields substantively, from solar coronal fields through star formation to galaxy clusters, so the 2015 working group's subject has become a cross-cutting concern of the whole book. The Cradle of Life, in contrast, remains small, and its chapters now divide between stellar formation and the solar system.
