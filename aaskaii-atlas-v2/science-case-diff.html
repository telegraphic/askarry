The 2015 book, Advancing Astrophysics with the Square Kilometre Array, was organised by science working group. Its successor is organised by astrophysical theme. This page asks where each 2015 working-group topic now appears, by counting the 2026 chapters that discuss it in substance.

What moved

Every 2015 topic still appears in the 2026 book, which shows that the reorganisation regrouped the science case rather than discarding parts of it. Many topics have one main home. Cosmology and the Epoch of Reionisation now concentrate in The Cosmos, and the continuum universe in From the Milky Way to Distant Galaxies. Pulsar physics and transients sit mainly in The Extreme Universe. In contrast, the Hydrogen Universe divides almost evenly between galaxy evolution and cosmology. This split reflects the difference between resolved HI studies of galaxies and HI intensity mapping of large-scale structure.

Magnetism is the clearest case of a topic that has spread rather than moved. Chapters in every 2026 section discuss magnetic fields in substance, from the solar corona through star formation to galaxy clusters. The subject of the 2015 working group has therefore become a concern of the whole book. The Cradle of Life, in contrast, remains small, and its chapters now divide between stellar formation and the solar system. Both sides of the comparison use the books' own sections, and the foot of the page lists the search terms behind each topic.
