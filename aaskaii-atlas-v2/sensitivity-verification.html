Sensitivity Verification

When a chapter states a sensitivity together with the observing parameters behind it, the figure can be recomputed. This page sends every such requirement to the live SKAO sensitivity calculator and reports whether the chapter's number survives. Because chapters rarely say which weighting scheme they assumed, each check tries all of them.

Why figures diverge

The divergences share a few causes, which the checking agent recorded row by row. The first is weighting. Four chapters reproduce closely under a weighting other than the one they state, which suggests that they did not in fact use the stated weighting. The second is the chapters' own models. Some chapters computed their noise from their own assumptions about system temperature or effective area. The two largest divergences, by factors of eight and forty, arise where those assumptions depart from the calculator's model of the array. The third is the calculator's limits, which a chapter may not have faced. The mixed AA* array accepts at most 720 MHz of Band 2 bandwidth, zoom channels stop at 13.44 kHz, and tapers come in fixed steps. Several checks therefore had to approximate the chapter's setup, and their notes say so.
