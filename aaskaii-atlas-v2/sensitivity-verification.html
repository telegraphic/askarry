Sensitivity Verification

Where a chapter states a sensitivity together with the observing parameters behind it, the figure can be recomputed. This page sends every such requirement in the register to the live SKAO sensitivity calculator, sweeping the weighting schemes because chapters rarely state which one they assumed, and reports whether the chapter's number survives.

What the check shows

Why figures diverge

The divergences share a small number of causes, which the checking agent recorded row by row. One is weighting: four chapters reproduce closely under a weighting other than the one they state, which suggests that the stated weighting was not the one used. A second group of chapters computed their noise from their own assumptions about system temperature or effective area, and the two largest divergences, by factors of eight and forty, arise where those assumptions depart from the calculator's model of the array. Finally, the calculator imposes limits that a chapter may not have faced: the mixed AA* array refuses more than 720 MHz of Band 2 bandwidth, zoom channels stop at 13.44 kHz, and tapers come in fixed steps, so several checks had to approximate the chapter's setup and say so.
