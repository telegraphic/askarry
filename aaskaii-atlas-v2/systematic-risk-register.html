Systematic Risk Register

Engineering and calibration risks threaten data quality regardless of which science question a chapter pursues. The register counts, for sixteen named risks, how many chapters in each book section discuss them at least twice, and ranks them by breadth across sections.

Chapters per section

Each cell gives the number of chapters in that section that name the risk at least twice; hover a cell for the section's size.

How to read the counts

A mention count is not a judgement that a chapter treats the risk as a binding constraint; it is the evidence from which such a judgement would start. The register therefore reports breadth and leaves severity to the chapters themselves, which a reader can open from the directory. Two terms were added to the fourteen that version 1 named in its method note (primary-beam modelling and bandpass or gain stability), bringing the list to the sixteen it claimed. Commensal scheduling conflict registers no chapter at the two-mention threshold, which confirms version 1's finding that the book does not treat it as a named risk.

What changed from v1: v1 excluded engineering documents by hand and could not confirm four risks; this scan covers only the AASKAII book, so the exclusion is automatic.
