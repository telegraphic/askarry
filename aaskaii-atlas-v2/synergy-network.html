Synergy Network

The network records which external facilities the AASKAII chapters name, in how many of the seven book sections, and which facilities appear together in the same chapters. Every node and edge is counted directly from the text, which replaces the version 1 graph that had been reconstructed from a written summary of another conversation.

What the counts show

The SKA precursors and pathfinders dominate the network, as one would expect of a book written by the communities that operate them: MeerKAT and LOFAR are each named by a large share of chapters in every section, and they share more chapters with each other than any other pair. Beyond radio astronomy, the structure is more informative. JWST, Planck and Euclid each appear in about forty chapters, but Planck and Euclid are concentrated in the cosmology chapters, whereas Rubin/LSST is the only facility outside radio astronomy named in all seven sections. Gaia shows the same pattern at smaller scale: with fifteen chapters, little more than a third as many as JWST, it matches JWST's reach of six sections, which marks it as a facility whose science touches most parts of the book rather than one community in depth.

The two gaps that version 1 flagged are now closed by measurement rather than by argument. The ngVLA is named in ten chapters across three sections, chiefly in galaxy-evolution and extreme-universe work, and the gamma-ray facilities are present as well: CTAO appears in twelve chapters over five sections and Fermi in six over three, with the extreme-universe section accounting for most of both. Consequently, these facilities belong in the synergy picture even though version 1 could not show them. Conversely, a handful of facilities on the curated list of seventy-eight are named by no chapter at the two-mention threshold, and they are listed at the foot of the page so that their absence is recorded rather than implied.
