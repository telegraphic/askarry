A 100-Hour HI Observing Proposal

A single prompt produced the draft below: "Write an observing proposal for a small 100-hour program looking at HI in galaxies using AA*". The draft drew on the AASKAII corpus and the live sensitivity calculator. This page reruns its figures through the calculator and the setup validator, and the sensitivity budget survives the check.

The science case

Neutral hydrogen extends farther from a galaxy than any other gas and is more easily disturbed. It is therefore the earliest tracer of tidal and ram-pressure stripping as a galaxy falls into a cluster. The draft requests resolved 21-cm imaging of twelve galaxies in the outskirts and infall region of the Fornax cluster, at a distance of about 20 Mpc. Its aim is to map tidal tails, warps and gas tails with better resolution and depth than MeerKAT, ASKAP and WSRT have reached. The sample is illustrative rather than pre-selected, and the investigator fields are placeholders. The page therefore presents the draft as a demonstration of what the corpus supports, not as a vetted request.

What the check shows

The draft's central number holds. The setup uses the AA* inner 2-km subarray, a 4 MHz zoom window of 13.44 kHz channels and six hours on source. For this setup the calculator returns 250 µJy/beam per channel at robust 0, with a 15.4″ × 12.5″ beam. Both figures lie within two per cent of the draft's 254 µJy/beam and its quoted beam. No other weighting comes within ten per cent, which confirms that the draft assumed robust 0, even though it names the weighting only in passing. The smoothed noise and the column-density limit follow from that figure by standard scaling, and both reproduce.

The setup is valid in the SV-AA*, Cycle 0 and Cycle 1 contexts. It is not valid in SV-AA2, because zoom mode is unavailable there and the inner 2-km template does not yet exist. Consequently, the programme could run only once AA* science verification begins. Two items remain unchecked. First, the HI-mass limit depends on a line width that the draft does not state. Second, the draft defines no output data products, so any estimate of data volume would have to invent them.

The check also taught one practical lesson for users of the calculator tools. The calculator's zoom endpoint accepts the subarray label, "AA* (MID_inner_r2km)". It refuses the subarray name, "MID_inner_r2km_aastar", with a validation error, even though its subarray list returns both.
